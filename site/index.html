ATRIL

ATRIL is a personal desktop application (Windows) that helps its owner prepare, fact-check, review and upload videos to their own YouTube channel. Every video requires the owner's explicit approval before it is published.

How ATRIL uses Google data

With the channel owner's consent, ATRIL uploads videos and thumbnails the owner approved and reads the owner's own channel analytics. All data stays on the owner's computer.
